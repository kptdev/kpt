kpt is organized around **packages** of Kubernetes Resource Model (KRM) configuration.
This chapter explains what a [package]({{% relref "/documentation/2-concepts/packages" %}}) is,
the typical [workflows]({{% relref "/documentation/2-concepts/workflows" %}}) for working with one,
and how [functions]({{% relref "/documentation/2-concepts/functions" %}}) automate mutation and
validation of the resources in it.
